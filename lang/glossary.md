# Zekra glossary

One word per concept, per language, everywhere: web console, mobile, desktop, browser extension,
emails, push notifications, store listings and zekra.dev. Translators (human or machine) pick
from this table; reviewers fix the table first, then the catalogs.

**Status:** English and Arabic are written by the team. Every other language is
**machine-assisted** (drafted 2026-10-14) and waits for a native-speaker review. Catalogs
carry the same note in their `"//"` key. See `docs/i18n.md` for the review workflow.

## Rules for every language

- **Never translated:** `Zekra` (the product, always Latin script; Arabic alone uses its own
  name, ذكرى), `MCP`, `OAuth`, `API`, `CLI`, `Markdown`, `PDF`, `Word`, product names
  (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, Gemini, Notion, Apple Notes, Google Keep, GitHub),
  tool names (`memory_recall`, `brain_chat`…), file extensions, URLs, keyboard shortcuts, code.
- **Brand terms** — Brain, Memory, Recall, Vault — are Zekra's own nouns. Each language picks
  ONE natural word for each (as Arabic uses دماغ, ذاكرة, تذكّر, خزنة) and keeps it everywhere;
  a language may keep the English word where that reads naturally to its users. The choice
  is recorded in the table below.
- **Placeholders** (`{name}`, `{count}`) are copied exactly, never translated. ICU plurals
  (`{count, plural, one {…} other {…}}`) keep their keywords; a translation may turn a bare
  `{count}` into a plural form for its language (ru/uk/pl/ar need it). Every plural and select
  keeps an `other` branch. `node scripts/i18n/check.mjs` fails CI otherwise.
- **Markup** such as `<b>…</b>` survives translation. `…` (ellipsis) on a menu item means
  "opens a dialog" — keep it.
- **Voice:** short, calm, direct. Buttons are verbs. No exclamation marks.
